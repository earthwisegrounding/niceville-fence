About Niceville Fence

A fence is the first thing people notice. We build it to last.

Niceville Fence is a locally based fence contractor working out of County Line Road. We design and install privacy, decorative and security fencing for homes and businesses across Niceville and the surrounding communities.

Every project starts with a visit to your property — we walk the line with you, talk through materials and heights, and give you a clear, written estimate at no cost.

How it works

Simple from first call to final gate.

No guesswork and no surprises. Here's what working with us looks like.

01

Call or request

Tell us about your property and what you'd like the fence to do — privacy, pets, pool or security.

02

On-site visit

We measure the line, look at grade and gates, and walk you through materials that suit the space.

03

Written estimate

You get a clear, itemized price at no cost — so you know exactly what's included before work begins.

04

Install & walkthrough

Our crew installs, cleans up, and walks the finished fence with you to make sure it's exactly right.
